ICS v3.9.1 — Spotlight Production Model Alignment
4 August 2026

PURPOSE
Align the working v3.9.0 Issue Canvas with the manually proved Spotlight publishing model without redesigning the system.

CHANGED
- Default Smart Issue Plan now proposes 9 Master Articles (normal editorial range 8–10), in three resumable batches of 3.
- Normal Spotlight issue range changed from 28–32 / 12–15 articles to about 24–30 sections / 8–10 concise Master Articles.
- Auto assembly now defaults to 26 sections and up to 9 ready Master Articles.
- 30-Block terminology replaced by Issue Mix / Issue Balance language.
- Commercial balance no longer demands arbitrary 5–8 partner lanes. It reports real attachments, activations and future opportunities.
- CTA controls are hidden for passive components unless the component/action actually warrants one.
- Life Lane inference aligned to the 12 shared Spotlight Life Lanes.
- Final QA updated to the new issue architecture.
- Server-side batch planner honours requested batch size and no longer hardcodes an expected 5.

UNCHANGED / PROTECTED
- Existing Canvas records and Component IDs.
- Drag/drop and move controls.
- Component Command Centre lifecycle.
- Research / Master Article production engine.
- Partner attachment model and conditional partner fields.
- Letterman packaging workflow.
- Airtable schema.

NEXT PROOF
Run one complete Norfolk Spotlight issue through Canvas, then PBS, then one niche title. Fix only production blockers before expanding Archive Intelligence.
