ICS v3.9.3 — Commercial Opportunity + Issue Balance Patch
4 August 2026

PURPOSE
Keep commercial value obvious without forcing arbitrary sponsor quotas, and stop a strong Spotlight plan becoming too civic/serious.

CHANGES
- Replaces legacy 5–8 Resident Expert / Featured Partner quota warnings with opportunity mapping.
- Removes legacy 30-Block Commercial Completion target logic.
- Renames the second balance action to Review Commercial Opportunities.
- Shows commercial pathways on every planned Master Article where they naturally exist.
- Issue audit reports number of Master Articles with natural commercial pathways, expert/authority lanes and feature/activation lanes.
- Commercial safeguards: real partner commitments can raise priority, but never lower the editorial floor; components can also fulfil partner value.
- Adds Life Lane spread check.
- Adds civic/public-service dominance check (normally no more than about 4 of 9 Master Articles unless the news week genuinely warrants it).
- Flags an overly practical/problem-heavy Master Article layer and asks for discovery, recommendation, human or enjoyment pieces.
- Keeps Unfiltered/debate and engagement variety checks without requiring every article to be question-led.
- Planner prompts now explicitly identify commercial routes and require at least two lighter/discovery/people-led Master Articles.
- Final QA recognises commercial opportunity visibility without demanding a sponsor quota.
- Removes leftover 28–32 / 12–15 line from generated Issue Build Pack.
