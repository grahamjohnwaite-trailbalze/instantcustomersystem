ICS v3.10.2 — SIGNAL POOL EXPANSION

Built from v3.10.1 Freshness + Archive Guard.

CHANGES
- Collect Signals now runs four editorial discovery passes instead of one:
  1. CURRENT — timely developments/events/changes
  2. LIFE — food, hospitality, places, days out, recommendations
  3. HUMAN — community, families, curiosity, fun, reader voice
  4. EVERGREEN — seasonal and evergreen reader questions
- Results are merged and semantically-light deduplicated client-side.
- Candidate pool is capped at 40 distinct leads.
- Planning now requires at least 18 distinct signals rather than 5.
- Collector clearly warns when fewer than 18 survive dedupe.
- Successful pools are preserved even if one discovery pass fails.
- Existing v3.10.1 Freshness + Archive Guard remains active.

IMPORTANT
This expands the live discovery pool. It does NOT yet claim the full 6,000+ Trail Blaze idea bank is wired into Airtable retrieval; that is a separate integration step.
