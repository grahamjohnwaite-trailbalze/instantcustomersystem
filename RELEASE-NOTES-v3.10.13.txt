ICS v3.10.13 — Open Question Gate Phrase Fix

Fixes a remaining false research block for open reader-question articles.

- Treats reader/resident nomination or testimony as optional when verified local examples already exist.
- Treats comparative proof of a “most changed” winner as optional when the article is framed as an open question rather than a definitive ranking.
- Treats independent proof of everyday-life impact as optional when the article only presents verified changes as discussion starters and does not claim proven impact.
- Keeps hard factual claims, definitive winners, invented consensus and unsupported outcome claims blocked.
