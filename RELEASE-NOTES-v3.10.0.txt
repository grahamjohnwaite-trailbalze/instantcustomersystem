ICS v3.10.0 — UNIVERSAL PLANNING ENGINE / EDITORIAL BALANCE GATE

BASELINE
- Built from clean v3.9.16 Decisive Official Source Gate.
- Does not include the failed v3.9.17–v3.9.21 Short Brief experiments.

WHAT THIS BUILD CHANGES
1. One universal planning engine with publication-specific Engine Profiles.
2. Auto profile selection from Publication DNA/name.
3. Initial profiles included:
   - Spotlight
   - Home Seller Insider
   - Taste Trail
   - Pet Insider
4. Spotlight now models broad editorial lanes before production:
   Local Life; Food & Hospitality; What's On; Places & Discovery; Money & Consumer;
   Home & Property; Family & Community; Motoring & Travel; Fun & Curiosity;
   Reader Voice; Business & People; Topical / Breaking.
5. Mood classification is separate from subject classification.
6. Planning Gate projects the complete issue (Master Articles + planned support/life components)
   before Airtable briefs are created.
7. Spotlight planning safeguards now include:
   - normal 24–30 section projected issue
   - 8–10 Master Articles
   - minimum Food/Hospitality representation
   - minimum What's On/Discovery representation
   - minimum Local Life/Community representation
   - minimum Fun/Curiosity representation
   - minimum Reader Interaction representation
   - heavy SAVE MONEY / PROBLEM SOLVING / URGENT mood capped at 40%
   - explicit warning when the issue lacks enough ENJOY / DISCOVER / CURIOUS / HUMAN moments
8. Plan lock is blocked when the projected editorial shape fails the planning gate.
9. Canonical Spotlight support components added, including Local Trivia, What's On, Food Find,
   Pub / Restaurant Question, Worth The Drive?, Sally Savers, Local Debate, Weekend Idea,
   Local Business Find, Nomination, Humour, Expert Quick Check and Before You Go.
10. Smart assembly now uses the active Engine Profile support recipe rather than the old fixed
    generic component sequence and fixed standalone partner blocks.
11. Engine Profile panel shows target sections, target Master Articles, support blocks, editorial
    lanes and current planning-gate health.
12. Commercial opportunity remains visible but can no longer by itself make a flat issue look balanced.

IMPORTANT SCOPE
- v3.10.0 is Phase 1: planning architecture and editorial balance.
- Spotlight is the first live acceptance profile.
- Niche profiles are present in the same engine architecture, but niche backend planning acceptance
  still needs to be proven after Spotlight passes.
- Short Brief persistence, one-source status sync, full-body Final QA and one-click Letterman are
  intentionally NOT changed in this build. Those follow after the planning engine is proven.
