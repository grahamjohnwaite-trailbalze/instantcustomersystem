ICS Issue Canvas v3.4.64 — Bounded Research Recovery

Built directly from v3.4.63.

- Targeted web recovery has a hard ~28-second budget.
- If evidence is still insufficient after recovery, production stops BEFORE Writer.
- The article is classified using the existing v3.4.63 evidence outcomes:
  SOURCE_CHECK_REQUIRED or RESEARCH_INCOMPLETE.
- Evidence found so far and exact missing-evidence reasons are retained.
- Produce Selected can be used later to retry research; insufficient checkpoints are not treated as reusable success.
- Evidence standards are unchanged.
- Difficult searches can no longer burn ~55 seconds on recovery and then another full Writer call for an article that still cannot pass.
