ICS v3.10.20 — STORY REPLACEMENT RESET + VISIBLE WRITING MODE

Fixes the Article 08 replacement workflow exposed in Norfolk Spotlight Issue #12.

Changes
- Generate section intelligence now detects when the operator has replaced a story by changing its title/core reader question.
- On a genuine story replacement, stale story-specific intelligence is cleared BEFORE regeneration: old reader problem, audience, proof/evidence requirements, source link, commercial pathway, CTA/action, social question, archive note, QA/final copy and research state.
- Keeps the issue running-order marker but replaces old Smart Plan story notes with a clean manual-replacement marker.
- Adds a visible Writing Mode control to the detailed section editor: AUTO, Conversation Advice, News Explainer, Debate / Controversy, Recommendation / Discovery, Practical Service, Comparison / Value, Human / Community, Breaking Update.
- Writing Mode persists inside Notes so no new Airtable field is required.
- Expands finance recognition to Junior ISA, savings, investing, university fund, Premium Bonds and family-finance questions.
- Family/university saving questions now generate appropriate parent audience, worked-example evidence needs, financial-adviser pathway and social question rather than stale local-planning language.
- National financial rules are not forced to use fake Norfolk local proof; the builder asks for a realistic worked example and authoritative UK evidence instead.

Operator workflow
1. Change Section Title and Core Reader Question.
2. Click Generate section intelligence.
3. ICS detects the replacement, clears the previous story intelligence and rebuilds the brief.
4. Review Writing Mode and regenerated fields.
5. Save section.
6. Research current article.
