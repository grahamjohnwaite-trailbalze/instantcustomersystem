ICS v3.22.0 — CLEAN PRODUCTION CORE

ACTIVE PATH
Plan → Approve → Research → Produce → Bank + Select → Coverage + Components → Assemble → QA → Letterman.

CORE RULES
- One active article type with Article Length Class FULL or SHORT.
- One persisted current-issue queue derived from the locked plan; REUSE assets stay in the Article Bank and are excluded from duplicate research/production.
- Evidence is a separate axis: VERIFIED / ATTRIBUTED / EDITORIAL / NEEDS_EVIDENCE.
- VERIFIED, ATTRIBUTED and EDITORIAL Research Packs are writeable; evidence exceptions are isolated.
- Legacy Feature sidecar is removed from the active workflow/UI.
- Evidence repair reruns only exception articles and forces fresh research; successful Research Packs are never rerun.
- Step 4 writes only from saved writeable Research Packs and never reruns research.
- Bulk writer uses a bounded two-worker pool, with a persistent live ticker and up to 14 minutes for a background writer to persist a terminal result.
- If fewer than the publication minimum can be written, Step 4 explicitly returns to the evidence exceptions as a named downstream blocker.
- No transient confirmation dialog before bulk writing; the persistent action/status strip is the operator control.

TESTS
- 20 approved with one REUSE => exactly 19 research jobs.
- VERIFIED / ATTRIBUTED / EDITORIAL => WRITEABLE.
- BLOCKED => NEEDS_EVIDENCE.
- QA-ready and next-step state transitions tested.
