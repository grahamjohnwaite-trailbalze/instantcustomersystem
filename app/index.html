Reconcile Letterman Articles

Tell ICS which Master Articles already exist in Letterman. This does not rewrite articles. It records the real published URL, optional CTA destination and — for legacy items only — whether Letterman is the canonical article copy.
Identity rule: use “Letterman copy is canonical” only when the article already published in Letterman is the version you intend to keep. ICS will then stop exporting a conflicting legacy body and will use the published URL as the source of truth for that article.
