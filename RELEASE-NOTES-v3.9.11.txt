ICS / TBOS v3.9.11 — CORE EVIDENCE GATE
4 August 2026

PURPOSE
Stop useful service/guide articles being blocked by evidence demands that are not necessary to answer the approved reader question.

FIXED
1. Core claim vs enrichment
   - Strong official/primary evidence can now satisfy a general service/advice article even when optional hands-on or local-enrichment checks are missing.
   - Missing evidence is only publication-blocking when it is needed for a material claim the article actually intends to make.

2. No unnecessary hands-on gate
   - Device testing, representative-bank testing and similar real-world recreation are non-blocking unless the approved brief actually depends on such testing.

3. Local proof is claim-aware
   - Norfolk-specific retailer/operator confirmation is required only when the article intends to name or make claims about those local entities.
   - A general UK factual/service answer is not blocked merely because it lacks an unnecessary Norfolk retailer example.

4. Discovery source is provenance, not a mandatory dependency
   - For guide/advice questions, inability to reopen the original news/RSS article does not block writing when authoritative independent sources answer the approved question.

5. Safety retained
   - Breaking/company-specific claims such as insolvency, liquidation, court, crash, closure and similar event-status stories do NOT receive the relaxed gate.
   - They still require direct verification or explicit source-check handling.
