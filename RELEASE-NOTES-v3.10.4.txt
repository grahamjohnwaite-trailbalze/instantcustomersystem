ICS v3.10.4 — Hybrid Signal + Idea Pool

Why:
The collect-issue-signals backend consistently returns six distinct live leads. Repeated client calls cannot manufacture breadth when the server response is capped/repetitive.

Change:
- Treat live discovery signals and evergreen editorial ideas as two different inputs.
- Preserve the six genuine/current signals.
- Supplement them to a 24-candidate planning pool with archive-guarded TBOS evergreen question seeds.
- Every evergreen seed is clearly labelled EDITORIAL_SEED / EVERGREEN_BANK and carries no factual claim or source URL.
- Evergreen seeds must be researched and localised before production.
- Recent same-publication history still blocks matching seeds.
- The 18-candidate planner gate now applies to the combined planning pool, not to current-news signals alone.
- Spotlight and initial niche profiles receive different evergreen seed sets.

Important:
This is an interim architecture correction, not a claim that the user's full 6,000+ idea bank is yet connected. The next data-layer step is to ingest/connect that bank and replace/extend the built-in seed set.
