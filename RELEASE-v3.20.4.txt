ICS v3.20.4 — Feature Article + Rhythm-Aware Assembly Engine
13 August 2026

PURPOSE
Fix the repeated CTT / CBS / PBS failure where Step 7 front-loaded substantial articles and left a long tail of lightweight components.

WHAT CHANGED
1. New permanent FEATURE ARTICLE content class.
   - Step 6A builds 4 short Feature Articles per issue.
   - Target length: 300–450 words.
   - Each Feature is independently researched through the existing evidence pipeline.
   - Each gets the normal Letterman article package: SEO, slug, expected URL, image brief, newsletter teaser and socials.
   - Features are Article Bank assets, not disposable newsletter filler.

2. Three editorial weights now exist.
   - MASTER ARTICLE: substantive permanent article.
   - FEATURE ARTICLE: 300–450 word permanent mid-weight article.
   - COMPONENT: short pace/personality/interaction block.

3. Step 6 component count is now calculated AFTER READY Feature Articles.
   Example Spotlight issue: 8 Masters + 4 Features + 15 components = 27 sections.
   Existing surplus component records are preserved but only the active target set is used; opening/closing roles are retained.

4. Step 7 is rhythm-aware.
   - Masters and Features are distributed across the whole issue.
   - Maximum two lightweight components in a row.
   - A substantial article anchor must appear in every third of the issue.
   - No support-only tail is allowed.
   - Step 7 cannot go green if the rhythm gate fails.

5. Universal reader standard remains locked.
   If it does not entertain, reward, surprise, help, tempt, amuse or make people want to read on, it does not get in.
   Earn attention before asking for comments, votes, replies or shares.

6. Article Bank now recognises both Masters and Feature Articles as permanent assets.

OPERATOR PATH FOR AN EXISTING ISSUE
Step 6 -> Build / Resume 4 Feature Articles -> let all four research/write -> build/repair remaining supporting components if required -> Step 7 Assemble.
Do not alter the saved Step 5 Master selection unless ICS identifies a specific Master defect.
