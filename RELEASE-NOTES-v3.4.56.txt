ICS Issue Canvas v3.4.56 — Safe Duplicate Retry

Built directly from the stable v3.4.54 package.

Only Replace behaviour changed:
- Other 14 article source leads are explicitly excluded from the replacement request.
- Current plan titles/questions/source signals are passed to the planner as prior articles.
- Existing same-issue duplicate gate still validates the returned candidate.
- If the first candidate duplicates another current article, ICS retries once automatically.
- Retry includes the exact rejection reason and asks for a different source lead and subject.
- Failed candidates are never saved.
- Navigation, app start-up, localStorage keys, plan rendering, Canvas, production and Airtable logic are unchanged.
