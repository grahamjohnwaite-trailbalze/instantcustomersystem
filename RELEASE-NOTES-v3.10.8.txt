ICS v3.10.8 — Semantic Lane Validation + Sponsor Matrix Fix

Fixes exposed by the Norfolk Spotlight planning audit:
- Planner-supplied life_lane is no longer blindly trusted for Spotlight balance counts.
- Primary editorial lane is validated against the actual article subject/title/question.
- A wrongly-labelled attraction/day-out/business story can no longer inflate FOOD & HOSPITALITY simply because the planner stored the wrong lane.
- READER VOICE cannot become the primary lane for an otherwise normal local-life Master purely because it is phrased as a reader question.
- Core sponsor opportunity classification no longer reads life_lane/lifeLane, preventing editorial lane metadata from falsely lighting up commercial categories.
- Short component TYPE still contributes to sponsor visibility, so implied category coverage remains intact.
- Existing freshness, 13/13 sponsor-lane visibility, mood gate, lane caps and archive guards remain unchanged.

Acceptance target for the current Norfolk test plan:
- Food/Hospitality should fall toward the actual food subjects (normally 3 Masters + Food Find support, not 7–8 Masters).
- Business/People should be recognised where the subject is genuinely a local business/person story.
- Reader Voice should reflect dedicated engagement components rather than planner metadata.
