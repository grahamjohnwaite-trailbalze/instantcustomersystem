ICS Issue Canvas v3.4.43 — Smart Issue Creation
Built directly from the stable v3.4.42 Recovery Safe baseline.

What changed
- Auto Assemble is replaced in the main workflow by Smart Plan Issue.
- Smart Plan uses current web research plus the publication, send date, Issue Promise, optional editor signals and the existing Produced Article library.
- Exactly 15 proper Master Article opportunities are proposed before production.
- Every proposed article is classified REUSE, REFRESH or CREATE NEW.
- Existing articles are treated as a resource bank, not a quota to fill.
- Planning explicitly considers local news/events, national-to-local consequences, money/home/transport, food/leisure, community/service and an earned Unfiltered/challenge angle.
- The plan is shown for editorial review before Airtable records are created.
- Create Missing Article Briefs writes only REFRESH and CREATE NEW briefs to the current issue. REUSE remains linked to the existing article library.
- Existing Master Article production pipeline remains responsible for researching/writing the missing articles.
- Assemble Approved Plan refuses to assemble until all 15 planned articles are actually complete.
- Final assembly remains 30 blocks: 15 articles + 13 supporting components + 2 partner blocks.
- Supporting component labels now use the current publication/locality rather than hard-coded Peterborough wording.
- Recovery Mode / Export Canvas Backup from v3.4.42 remains intact.

Deliberately unchanged
- Navigation
- Canvas persistence/recovery
- Existing drag/drop implementation
- Master Article research/writer pipeline
- Partner assignment logic
- Final QA
