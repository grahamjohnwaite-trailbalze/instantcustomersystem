# ICS v3.23.0 — Research Yield Engine

Production-only overhaul aimed at the 25 → 20 READY contract.

- Curated source bank is now actively routed by article/life-lane intent, including local news, food, events, telecoms, safety, community and planning sources.
- Research query plans are intent-first rather than raw-title-first, preventing count words such as “Three” from becoming the subject of the search.
- Explicit drift guards reject dictionary pages and unrelated Three.com results.
- Research now carries a delivery contract: promised named examples, counts, current prices/dates/opening details and route facts must be found rather than rewritten into a generic guide.
- Recovery prompt explicitly starts with the curated source route and may replace a failed discovery lead with stronger current sources while preserving the approved reader question.
- No changes to saved READY articles, writer runtime, QA rules or issue selection state.
