ICS v3.10.5 — Life Lane + Commercial Coverage Guard

Purpose
- Stop Spotlight plans passing merely because they are fresh.
- Require the whole 24–30 section projected issue to cover the broader life mix and natural sponsor/expert territory.

Changes
- Spotlight whole-issue minimums now include:
  * 2+ Food / Hospitality
  * 2+ What's On / Discovery
  * 2+ Local Life / Community
  * 2+ Money / Home / Consumer
  * 1+ Motoring / Travel
  * 1+ Business / People
  * 2+ Fun / Curiosity
  * 3+ Reader interaction
- Added dedicated Motoring Quick Check and Home Quick Check support components so these lanes can be filled by short useful pieces rather than forcing more Master Articles.
- Spotlight support recipe remains 18 blocks and now deliberately covers the missing life lanes.
- Commercial balance guard now checks diversity across the issue rather than rewarding 9/9 Masters for having a nominal route.
- Target commercial shape: 4–8 distinct natural categories, including at least 2 expert/help lanes and 2 activation/featured-partner lanes.
- Over-engineered commercial coverage is flagged; editorial-first remains the rule.
- Planning gate treats missing required life/commercial coverage as NEEDS ATTENTION.
