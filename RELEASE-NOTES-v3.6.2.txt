Issue Canvas v3.6.2 — Evidence Classifier Fix

Based on: v3.6.1 Staged Research Lock
Rollback: v3.4.73 Stable

Objective
Correct the evidence decision logic without changing the staged research/generate architecture.

Changed
- VERIFIED_NOW now requires an official or primary source that directly verifies the article premise.
- Context-only official sources no longer trigger VERIFIED_NOW.
- Credible local reporting with incomplete official confirmation becomes ATTRIBUTED_REPORT.
- Added RESEARCH_INCOMPLETE for relevant but inadequate evidence.
- ATTRIBUTED_REPORT writer mode must explicitly attribute the unconfirmed central claim.
- Airtable evidence status remains Reported — Attribution Required after generation.
- Production Service markers updated to v3.6.2.

Regression test 2
PBS article directly supported by a matching official source: expected VERIFIED_NOW.

Regression test 3
Weak niche story with only contextual evidence and no credible report: expected RESEARCH_INCOMPLETE or BLOCKED.
