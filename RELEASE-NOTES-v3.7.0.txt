ISSUE CANVAS v3.7.0 — EDITORIAL BRAIN v1

PURPOSE
Turn locked research into human, engaging Spotlight articles without weakening factual discipline.

ADDED
- Editorial Brain planning inside the writer prompt.
- Human-story decisions before drafting: reader, emotion, tension, local relevance, practical payoff, share reason and conversation starter.
- Friend Test and Let-Them-In Test.
- Clear permission for fair editorial opinion when distinguished from fact.
- Stronger opening and specific discussion-ending rules.
- Editorial Strategy Card stored in the Master Article Package.
- Seven quality scores: stop-scroll, human readability, friend test, local identity, conversation, practical value and evidence discipline.
- Mandatory rewrite when key scores fall below 7.

UNCHANGED
- Two-stage Research then Generate workflow.
- Locked Research Pack.
- Evidence classifier and attribution rules.
- Completion detection.
- Airtable workflow and fields.

TEST
