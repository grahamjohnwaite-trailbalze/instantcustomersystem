ICS v3.9.9 — BULK PRODUCTION PIPELINE FIX

Purpose
Fix the bulk "Write all approved articles" workflow discovered during the Norfolk Issue 11 proof.

Problem confirmed
The bulk button called Master Article generation directly. For B/C evidence classes, produce-section correctly refused to write without a saved Research Pack, producing: "Research is not complete. Run Research selected article first."

Changes
1. Bulk production is now a staged two-step pipeline per queued article:
   Research -> save Research Pack/checkpoint -> Write Master Article.
2. VERIFIED_NOW and ATTRIBUTED_REPORT research outcomes may proceed to writing using the saved Research Pack.
3. BLOCKED / RESEARCH_INCOMPLETE / SOURCE_CHECK_REQUIRED stop that article before writing and continue the queue.
4. One article failure no longer stops the rest of the bulk queue.
5. Progress panel explicitly shows Step 1/2 Researching and Step 2/2 Writing.
6. Final production summary/status is rendered in the page and selectable/copyable.
7. Existing single-article Research and Write controls remain unchanged.
8. Existing v3.9.8 planner/reuse guard behaviour is preserved.

Acceptance test
Open Norfolk Spotlight Issue 11 in Build Issue and click Write all approved articles. The 9 queued articles should no longer all fail immediately with "Research is not complete". Each should first complete research; publishable research then proceeds to Master Article writing. Any blocked/research-incomplete item should be held for review while the remaining queue continues.
