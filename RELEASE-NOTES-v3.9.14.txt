ICS Issue Canvas v3.9.14 — Authoritative Source Override

Purpose
- Let an editor qualify a questionable article by adding one stronger official or primary source URL.
- Make that source part of the next research run instead of leaving it buried in Notes.

Changes
1. Added “Add / replace authoritative source URL” to the Current article workflow.
2. Pasting a URL saves it to Source / Reference Link 1 and adds a structured AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE UPDATE marker.
3. The existing Research Pack and Master Article are explicitly marked stale until research is rerun.
4. Research now fetches the editor-supplied URL directly and retains it for qualification before general search results.
5. The targeted research prompt is told to assess the exact editor URL first and extract what it confirms, does not confirm, dates, named bodies and action routes.
6. Recognises ABTA and key UK regulator/trade-body domains as authoritative sources.

SkiYodl acceptance test
- Select Article 1.
- Click Add / replace authoritative source URL.
- Paste https://www.abta.com/news/ski-yodl-ltd-advice-customers
- Click 1. Research current article.
- View research and confirm ABTA appears as a retained source and the liquidation/customer routes are extracted.
- Mark evidence verified when correct, then click 3. Write current Master Article.
- The rewritten article must not retain the old “liquidation unconfirmed” position.
