ICS Issue Canvas v3.4.49 — Fast Evidence Staging

Built directly from v3.4.48.

Master Article production change:
- Removes the long web-enabled OpenAI research call that repeatedly stalled.
- Uses fast article-specific Bing Web RSS + Google News RSS discovery queries.
- Queries include the approved title/question, current signal where available, and targeted official-domain searches.
- Official/local sources are ranked ahead of generic results.
- The resulting compact evidence pack is checkpointed to Airtable before Writer starts.
- Evidence-heavy articles still require at least two relevant source leads and at least one official/local source to pass the existing evidence gate.
- Writer is explicitly forbidden from expanding beyond the supplied source snippets.
- Weak evidence becomes Fix Required / human verification rather than invented certainty.
- Existing Writer checkpoint/retry logic, Smart Plan, Editorial Control and Recovery Mode are retained.

This is deliberately a reliability-first research layer. Final publication still requires the existing QA/evidence checks, especially for legal, medical, financial or contested claims.
