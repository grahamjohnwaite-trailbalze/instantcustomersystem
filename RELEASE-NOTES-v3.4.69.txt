ICS Issue Canvas v3.4.69 — Evidence Gate Classification

Built directly from v3.4.68.

Fixes the blocker demonstrated by Norfolk Article 7.

- Adds deterministic post-research evidence timing classification.
- Future results such as durability, repeat-repair rates, damage outcomes and savings are FUTURE TESTS, not publication blockers.
- Non-essential operational detail such as exact roads, contractors, equipment and timetable is OPTIONAL when the verified premise can be written without claiming those details.
- Genuine premise/identity/accountability gaps remain REQUIRED NOW.
- A story can proceed to Writer when:
  * the exact subject is resolved,
  * there are at least two article-specific sources,
  * the accountable body or strong primary evidence is identified,
  * no genuine required-now blocker remains.
- The stop-before-Writer gate now uses this publication decision instead of blindly trusting a research model's 'Insufficient' label.
- Duplicate exception reasons are de-duplicated.
- Source hierarchy, entity-first research, 55-second recovery, stable article identity and editorial question framing remain unchanged.
