ICS Issue Canvas v3.4.70 — Research/Writer Wiring

Exact fix for the stale Norfolk pothole article package.

Root cause:
- Recovery research found four current sources.
- The production gate used that recovered research.
- Writer reused an older saved checkpoint created from a zero-source pack because checkpoint reuse was matched only to article title/question.

Fix:
- Writer checkpoints are now bound to a deterministic fingerprint of the exact research pack.
- Any legacy Writer checkpoint or checkpoint from different sources, resolved entity, timing buckets or evidence summary is invalidated.
- Writer is then called again with the active recovered research object.
- Final QA uses the same editorial evidence outcome as the pre-Writer gate rather than reverting to the raw gate.pass flag.
- Only genuine required-now blockers are appended to the final exception/evidence summary.

Acceptance test:
- Re-run Norfolk Article 7 once.
- Trace should show Writer checkpoint invalidated, then Writer request.
- The new package must mention the resolved Norfolk County Council trial evidence and must not claim that zero sources were retained.
