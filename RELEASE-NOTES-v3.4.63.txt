ICS Issue Canvas v3.4.63 — Research Recovery & Evidence Classification

Built directly from stable v3.4.62.

Research recovery:
- Fast evidence collection remains the first pass.
- If evidence is insufficient, production automatically performs a targeted second live-web research pass before writing.
- Recovery explicitly seeks the named organisation/scheme/decision and primary or accountable sources.
- It resolves missing evidence rather than padding to a source-count target.
- Insufficient old research/writer checkpoints are NOT reused, so existing review articles can be rerun and genuinely re-researched.
- HTML is stripped from recovered source-support text.

Operator outcomes:
- COMPLETE
- SOURCE CHECK REQUIRED
- RESEARCH INCOMPLETE
- EDITORIAL REVIEW (reserved)
- FAILED

Build Issue now shows specific evidence outcomes instead of generic NEEDS REVIEW where v3.4.63 production has classified the reason.

Safety:
- Evidence gate is not weakened.
- High-stakes/current articles remain blocked when core evidence is unresolved.
- No existing Master Article/library record is deleted.
