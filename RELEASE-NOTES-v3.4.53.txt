ICS Issue Canvas v3.4.53 — Refresh & Duplication Gate

Built directly from v3.4.52.

- Every REFRESH is now tested for a material change since the prior article.
- 'Still relevant', 'it's August', 'evergreen', or generic ongoing reader need are not sufficient reasons to refresh.
- REFRESH TOO WEAK items are visibly flagged and block plan locking.
- Planner prompt requires a concrete new rule, decision, figure, price, policy, local development, evidence set or result that materially changes the answer.
- Recently covered subjects have a strong presumption against refresh unless the answer has materially changed.
- Existing v3.4.52 provenance/source alignment, v3.4.51 source precision, fast production, checkpoints and recovery remain unchanged.
