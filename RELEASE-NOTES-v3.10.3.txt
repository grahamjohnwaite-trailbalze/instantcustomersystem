ICS v3.10.3 — DIVERSE SIGNAL COLLECTOR

Purpose
Fix the v3.10.2 failure where four parallel discovery calls repeatedly returned the same six leads, leaving only six after dedupe.

Changes
- Signal discovery now runs sequentially rather than in parallel.
- Each discovery pass receives the leads already found and is explicitly told not to return or rephrase them.
- Existing saved leads are preserved on subsequent Collect Signals runs instead of being overwritten.
- Six distinct discovery pools: Current; Food & Hospitality; What's On & Discovery; Human & Community; Fun & Reader; Evergreen.
- Collection stops early once a healthy pool of 30 distinct candidates has been reached.
- Re-running Collect Signals grows the candidate pool because previously saved leads become exclusions for the next discovery run.
- The 18-lead planning minimum remains in place.

Acceptance test
Norfolk Spotlight should produce at least 18 distinct leads before planning, with visibly different subject matter across hospitality, discovery, local life, engagement and practical/current content.
