ISSUE CANVAS v3.6.0 — RESEARCH LOCK

BASE
- Built directly from proven v3.4.73 UK Source Precision.
- Rollback build: v3.4.73.

OBJECTIVE
Separate evidence decisions from article writing. Research is locked before the writer runs, and the writer cannot browse or change the evidence class.

ADDED
1. Locked Research Pack v2
   - persistent research_id
   - research_lock flag
   - locked_at timestamp
   - retained source and missing-evidence record

2. Evidence Engine v2
   - VERIFIED_NOW
   - ATTRIBUTED_REPORT
   - BLOCKED

3. Attributed writer mode
   - central reported claims must be attributed
   - absence of matching official confirmation must be stated
   - reported claims must not be upgraded into established facts

4. Editorial decision record
   - evidence outcome
   - Research Pack ID and lock state
   - human-review requirement
   - explicit confirmation that writer research is disabled

CHANGED
- Master Article Package upgraded to v2.
- Production Service upgraded to v3.0.
- Writer uses the locked Research Pack with useWeb:false.
- A credible local report may proceed as ATTRIBUTED_REPORT rather than being automatically blocked.
- Weak or unsafe evidence remains BLOCKED.

NOT CHANGED
- Issue planning
- drag/drop
- partner assignment
- components
- Master Content Ideas
- Archive Recovery
